3.6m x 2m LED Screen – P1.8 Hire

Home | Hire Audio Visual Products | Hire LED Screens & Processors Hire & Rental | 3.6m x 2m LED Screen – P1.8 Hire
Back

Get A Quote

A large-format LED screen available for hire, designed for exhibitions, events, and brand activations where maximum visual impact is required.

With a 1.8mm pixel pitch, this screen delivers sharp, high-definition content even at close viewing distances — ideal for detailed video, graphics, and branding.

At 3.6m wide by 2m high, it creates a strong centrepiece for exhibition stands, helping attract attention and showcase content clearly across larger spaces.

The high brightness output ensures visibility in busy, well-lit environments, while the seamless LED finish provides a clean, professional look.

This screen can be installed flush to a wall or built as a freestanding structure, depending on your stand design and layout.

Typical hire includes:

  • Delivery, setup, and collection
  • LED screen, structure, and processing
  • Cabling and power planning
  • On-site technician support (if required)

A powerful and flexible LED solution designed to maximise visibility and engagement.

Key Features:

  • 1.8mm pixel pitch for close-up clarity
  • High brightness for exhibition environments
  • Freestanding or wall-mounted options
