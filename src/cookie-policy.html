This Cookie Policy explains how GreenEFX Studio may use cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer, mobile device or browser when you visit a website. They help websites remember certain information and improve browsing functionality.

2. Why We Use Cookies

Depending on the technologies enabled on our website, cookies may help us:

  • Ensure website features function correctly.
  • Remember certain user preferences.
  • Understand website traffic and visitor interactions.
  • Improve website performance and user experience.
  • Measure marketing and advertising performance where applicable.

3. Types of Cookies

Essential Cookies

These cookies support basic website functionality and security. They may be necessary for certain website features to operate.

Functional Cookies

These cookies may remember user preferences and settings to provide a more convenient browsing experience.

Analytics Cookies

These cookies may help us understand visitor activity, page views and website performance when analytics services are enabled.

Marketing Cookies

These cookies may be used to measure advertising effectiveness or provide relevant marketing content when such tools are used.

Certain third-party tools or embedded services may use their own cookies. Their collection and use of information are governed by their respective policies.

5. Managing Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect some website features or functionality.

6. Consent and Preferences

Where required by applicable law, we will request consent before using non-essential cookies. Where a consent mechanism is provided, you may change or withdraw your preferences through that mechanism.

7. Data Protection

Information collected through cookies may be handled according to our Privacy Policy. We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through our website.
