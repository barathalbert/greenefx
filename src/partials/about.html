GreenEFX Media is a creative production and digital advertising company specializing in , Advertisement Film Shooting & Production, 3D Animation, Visual Effects (VFX), CGI Advertisements, Corporate Films, Digital Marketing, AI Video Production, and immersive visual experiences.

Our journey began in 2007 as a multimedia training institute in Vellore, Tamil Nadu. Over the years, we have evolved into a full-service creative production company, combining artistic excellence, advanced technology, and innovative storytelling to deliver impactful visual solutions.

From cinematic visual effects and photorealistic 3D animations to engaging brand campaigns and digital experiences, we help businesses transform their ideas into powerful visual communication.
