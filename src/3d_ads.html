About Our Medical Animation Services

Medical information can be difficult to communicate through text and static images alone. GreenEFX Media combines professional 3D modeling, animation, CGI, compositing and cinematic storytelling to create educational and promotional medical videos. Projects are developed from client-supplied clinical references and reviewed by designated medical subject-matter experts.
