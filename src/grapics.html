Graphic Design Course with AI Tools

Learn professional graphic design in our hands-on classes. Combine core design skills with industry-standard software and AI tools to create logos, posters, brand identities and marketing creatives.

Build a career in visual design with our practical, career-focused graphic design training. You will start with design fundamentals and move on to Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign, then learn quick-turnaround tools like Canva and AI image generators.
